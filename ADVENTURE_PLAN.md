Adventure Game Plan: Pirate Treasure

Game Overview
The player is a pirate searching for hidden treasure. One day, they find a treasure map that leads to a mysterious island. The player must make choices to find the treasure and avoid danger.

Story Paths:

Path 1: Enemy Pirates
The player meets a group of enemy pirates.
Decision 1:
- Choice A: Fight the pirates.
- Choice B: Run away.
Decision 2:
- Choice A: Take their treasure map.
- Choice B: Escape to the island.
Outcome: The player either gets a new map or escapes safely.


Path 2: The Island
The player arrives at the island where the treasure is hidden.
Decision 1:
- Choice A: Explore a cave.
- Choice B: Search the beach.
Decision 2:
- Choice A: Open a mysterious chest.
- Choice B: Follow footprints into the forest.
Outcome: The player finds a clue that leads to the treasure.


Path 3: The Storm
A storm appears while the player sails toward the island.
Decision 1:
- Choice A: Sail through the storm.
- Choice B: Find shelter.
Decision 2:
- Choice A: Repair the ship.
- Choice B: Use a lifeboat to reach the island.
Outcome: The player survives the storm and reaches the island.


Ending:
No matter which path the player chooses, they eventually reach the treasure chest. When they open it, they discover a pile of gold and jewels. The player wins the adventure.

Story Branches:
The game starts with the player finding a treasure map. They choose to face enemy pirates, explore the island, or survive a storm. Each path has two decisions. All paths eventually lead to the treasure and the ending.
